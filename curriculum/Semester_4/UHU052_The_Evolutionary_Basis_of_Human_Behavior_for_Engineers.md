# UHU052: The Evolutionary Basis of Human Behavior for Engineers
**Semester**: Semester IV | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: HSS | **L-T-P**: 1-0-0 | **Credits**: 1.0 | **Contact Hours**: 1

## 🎯 Course Objective
To introduce foundational concepts of evolutionary psychology and behavioral sciences to engineering students, facilitating better understanding of team collaboration, leadership, and human-centered design.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Understand evolutionary principles influencing human behavior and cognition.
- **CLO2**: Analyze social dynamics, cooperation, and group decision-making in technical teams.
- **CLO3**: Apply behavioral insights to human-centered engineering design and workplace ethics.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Evolutionary psychology foundations, cognitive adaptations, cooperation vs competition in groups, social hierarchy and leadership, behavioral decision-making in high-stakes engineering projects.

## 📖 Prescribed Text Books & References
1. Buss, David M., Evolutionary Psychology: The New Science of the Mind, Routledge (2019).
