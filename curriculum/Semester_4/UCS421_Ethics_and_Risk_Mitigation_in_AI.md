# UCS421: Ethics and Risk Mitigation in AI
**Semester**: Semester IV | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: ESC | **L-T-P**: 3-0-0 | **Credits**: 3.0 | **Contact Hours**: 3

## 🎯 Course Objective
To examine ethical, legal, and safety risks in artificial intelligence systems and formulate algorithmic safeguards, compliance mechanisms, and transparent governance models.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Identify ethical challenges, algorithmic biases, and societal impacts in AI deployments.
- **CLO2**: Apply regulatory frameworks, governance models, and data privacy principles to AI systems.
- **CLO3**: Formulate risk mitigation strategies, fairness audits, and accountability mechanisms for AI engineering.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- AI safety, fairness, accountability, transparency (FAT), algorithmic bias, privacy, intellectual property, deepfakes, autonomous systems liability, IEEE 7000-series standards.

## 📖 Prescribed Text Books & References
1. Russell, Stuart, Human Compatible: Artificial Intelligence and the Problem of Control, Viking (2019).
1. Floridi, Luciano, The Ethics of Information, Oxford University Press (2013).
