# UEC612: Digital System Design
**Semester**: Semester IV | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: PCC | **L-T-P**: 3-1-2 | **Credits**: 4.5 | **Contact Hours**: 6

## 🎯 Course Objective
To familiarize the student with analysis, design and evaluation of digital systems of medium complexity based on SSI, MSI and Programmable logic devices, timing analysis of synchronous and asynchronous systems, and HDL synthesis.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Apply various minimization methods to simplify boolean functions and construct circuits using logic gates.
- **CLO2**: Design combinational circuits and iterative networks using basic modules.
- **CLO3**: Design and analyze sequential circuits and finite state machines.
- **CLO4**: Evaluate performance of integrated circuits across logic families.
- **CLO5**: Design, simulate and synthesize digital systems using HDL (VHDL/Verilog).

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Binary Codes & Combinational Design: Review of binary codes, error detection/correction. Quine-McCluskey, adders, subtractors, multiplexers, decoders, ROM, PLA, PAL.
- Sequential Circuits: Latches, flip-flops, universal shift registers, counters, Static Timing Analysis, FSMs (Moore & Mealy), race and hazards, hazard-free design.
- Logic Families: TTL, CMOS logic families, noise margin, propagation delay, power dissipation, interfacing TTL & CMOS.
- Hardware Description Languages: VHDL/Verilog modeling styles: Dataflow, Behavioral, Structural, simulation and synthesis.
- Standards Familiarization: IEEE 91a-1991, IEEE 91-1984.
