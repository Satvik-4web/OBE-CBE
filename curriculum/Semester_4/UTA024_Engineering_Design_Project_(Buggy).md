# UTA024: Engineering Design Project (Buggy)
**Semester**: Semester IV | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: ESC | **L-T-P**: 1-0-2 | **Credits**: 2.0 | **Contact Hours**: 3

## 🎯 Course Objective
The project introduces students to the challenge of electronic systems design & integration, hardware-software co-design, sensor interfacing, and coordinated team engineering.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Recognize issues to be addressed in combined hardware and software system design.
- **CLO2**: Draw schematic diagrams of electronic circuits and design PCB layouts using CAD tools.
- **CLO3**: Apply hands-on experience in electronic circuit implementation and testing.
- **CLO4**: Demonstrate programming skills by integrating coding, optimization and debugging for different robotic challenges.
- **CLO5**: Develop group working, including task sub-division and integration of individual contributions from the team.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Hardware Overview of Arduino: Microcontroller architecture, Eagle PCB layout tool, active and passive components.
- Sensors & Actuators: IR sensors, ultrasonic sensors, DC motors, H-bridge driver circuits.
- Programming & Integration: Embedded C/C++ firmware, C# application interface, serial communication, motor control algorithms.
- Buggy Challenges: Autonomous track navigation, obstacle avoidance, gantry communication, multi-robot coordination.
