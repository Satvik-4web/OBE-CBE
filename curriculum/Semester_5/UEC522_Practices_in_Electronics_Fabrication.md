# UEC522: Practices in Electronics Fabrication
**Semester**: Semester V | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: ESC | **L-T-P**: 1-0-2 | **Credits**: 2.0 | **Contact Hours**: 3

## 🎯 Course Objective
To provide fundamental understanding of integrated circuit (IC) fabrication and packaging technologies, crystal growth, planar processing, layout rules, and packaging.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Explain fundamentals of integrated circuits, semiconductor materials, crystal growth, and wafer preparation.
- **CLO2**: Apply principles of oxidation, diffusion, ion implantation, lithography, etching, and metallization to describe planar IC fabrication.
- **CLO3**: Analyse and apply various IC Design rules.
- **CLO4**: Understand Packaging technologies by considering electrical, thermal, and mechanical performance requirements.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Integrated Circuits: Evolution of IC technology, monolithic vs hybrid, SSI to ULSI, Moore's Law.
- Wafer Growth: Czochralski, Float Zone methods, slicing, polishing, chemical mechanical planarization.
- Planar Fabrication: Cleanroom technology, oxidation, diffusion, ion implantation, CVD, PVD, photolithography, etching (wet/dry), metallization.
- Design Rules & Packaging: Lambda design rules, aspect ratio, stick diagrams. IC packaging (DIP, QFP, BGA, CSP), wire bonding.
- Standards Familiarization: IEEE Std 255-1963, IEEE P1838, IEEE P370, IEEE 1149.1.
