# UEC523: Applications of AI for Electronic Systems
**Semester**: Semester V | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: PCC | **L-T-P**: 2-0-2 | **Credits**: 3.0 | **Contact Hours**: 4

## 🎯 Course Objective
To equip students with techniques for deploying artificial intelligence and machine learning algorithms onto resource-constrained embedded systems and electronic hardware.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Understand machine learning techniques tailored for electronic sensor data.
- **CLO2**: Implement TinyML and embedded AI models on microcontrollers and edge hardware.
- **CLO3**: Optimize neural network models for power, latency, and memory-constrained electronic systems.
- **CLO4**: Apply AI algorithms to fault detection, circuit optimization, and signal classification.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Edge AI foundations, TinyML, model quantization, pruning, microcontroller deployment (ARM Cortex-M), sensor fusion, predictive maintenance, automated circuit fault diagnosis.

## 📖 Prescribed Text Books & References
1. Warden, Pete and Situnayake, Daniel, TinyML: Machine Learning with TensorFlow Lite on Arduino and Ultra-Low-Power Microcontrollers, O'Reilly (2019).
