# UTA025: Innovation & Entrepreneurship
**Semester**: Semester V | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: HSS | **L-T-P**: 1-0-2 | **Credits**: 3.0 | **Contact Hours**: 3

## 🎯 Course Objective
To provide students with basic understanding of entrepreneurship, frameworks for opportunity discovery, business model design, and venture finance.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Explain fundamentals behind entrepreneurial personality and intentions.
- **CLO2**: Discover, evaluate, and validate business opportunities.
- **CLO3**: Identify stakeholders and develop strong value propositions.
- **CLO4**: Describe business models and construct a functional Business Model Canvas.
- **CLO5**: Analyse and select suitable finance and revenue models for startup ventures.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Introduction to Entrepreneurship: Personality, traits, entrepreneurial challenges.
- Opportunity Identification: Discovery vs creation, prototype and exemplar models, reverse engineering.
- Business Models & Lean Startups: Business Model Canvas, value proposition design, lean startup methodology, customer validation, pitching.
- Venture Finance: Sources of capital, bootstrap vs angel/VC funding, capitalization tables, financial forecasting.

## 📖 Prescribed Text Books & References
1. Ries, Eric, The Lean Startup, Penguin Books (2011).
1. Blank, Steve, The Startup Owner's Manual, K&S Ranch (2013).
