# UEC504: Signal System and Processing
**Semester**: Semester V | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: PCC | **L-T-P**: 3-1-2 | **Credits**: 4.5 | **Contact Hours**: 6

## 🎯 Course Objective
To develop analytical capability in continuous and discrete-time signals and systems, Fourier and Z-transforms, and designing FIR and IIR digital filters.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Analyze different properties of continuous and discrete-time signals and systems.
- **CLO2**: Represent signals and systems in frequency domain using Fourier tools.
- **CLO3**: Apply Z-transform to analyze discrete-time signals and systems.
- **CLO4**: Design and implement digital discrete-time filters (FIR/IIR).

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Signals & Systems Representation: Continuous and discrete-time signals, LTI systems, convolution, sampling theorem.
- Frequency Characterization: Continuous/discrete Fourier series and transforms, transfer function, impulse response.
- Z-Transform: Region of convergence, inverse Z-transform, difference equations.
- Discrete Fourier Transforms (DFT): Fast Fourier Transform (FFT), Radix-2 decimation in time/frequency.
- Digital Filter Design: FIR filter design using windowing, IIR filter design (Butterworth, Chebyshev), realization structures (Direct I/II, cascade, parallel).
- Standards Familiarization: IEEE 1641-2010, IEEE 21451-001-2017.
