# UCS321: AI for Engineers
**Semester**: Semester III | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: PCC | **L-T-P**: 2-0-2 | **Credits**: 3.0 | **Contact Hours**: 4

## 🎯 Course Objective
To introduce students to intelligent system concepts, heuristics, state space search, knowledge representation, and practical machine learning applications tailored for engineering disciplines.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Understand foundational artificial intelligence concepts, problem formulation, and search strategies.
- **CLO2**: Apply knowledge representation, logical reasoning, and probabilistic inference to engineering problems.
- **CLO3**: Implement supervised and unsupervised machine learning models using contemporary AI libraries.
- **CLO4**: Evaluate AI solutions on real-world engineering datasets considering performance and ethical dimensions.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Introduction to AI: Intelligent agents, environments, problem formulation, search algorithms (uninformed and informed search, A*, heuristic functions).
- Knowledge & Reasoning: Propositional and first-order logic, inference, ontological engineering, probabilistic reasoning, Bayesian networks.
- Learning Techniques: Supervised and unsupervised learning basics, decision trees, linear models, clustering, neural network intro.
- Applications & Ethics: AI applications in signal classification, computer vision, robotics, and ethical engineering considerations.
