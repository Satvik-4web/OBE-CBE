# UNC305: Discrete Mathematics
**Semester**: Semester III | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: BSC | **L-T-P**: 3-1-0 | **Credits**: 3.5 | **Contact Hours**: 4

## 🎯 Course Objective
To understand mathematical reasoning in order to read, comprehend and construct Mathematical arguments as well as to solve problems occurred in computer systems and networks.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Apply Knowledge of mathematics in truth tables and the rules of propositional & predicate calculus.
- **CLO2**: Apply the logical and analytical structure of proofs and work symbolically with connectives and quantifiers to produce logically valid arguments.
- **CLO3**: Evaluate elementary mathematical arguments and identify fallacious reasoning.
- **CLO4**: Analyze real-world problems using graph theory.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Basic Logic: Propositional logic, Truth tables, DNF, Inference rules, Universal and existential quantifiers.
- Sets, Relations and Functions: Set builder notation, Venn diagrams, Cartesian product, Power sets, Equivalence relations, Poset, Lattice, Hasse's Diagram, Injective and surjective functions, Composition.
- Proof Techniques: Direct proofs, Disproving by counterexample, Proof by contrapositive, Proof by contradiction, Mathematical Induction.
- Basics of Counting: Counting arguments, Sum and product rules, Pigeonhole principle, Permutations and combinations, Binomial theorem, Recurrence relations.
- Graph Theory: Undirected/directed graphs, Weighted graphs, Spanning trees, Graph isomorphism, Eulerian and Hamiltonian cycles, Planarity.
