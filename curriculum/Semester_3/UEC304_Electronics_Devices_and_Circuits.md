# UEC304: Electronics Devices and Circuits
**Semester**: Semester III | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: PCC | **L-T-P**: 3-1-2 | **Credits**: 4.5 | **Contact Hours**: 6

## 🎯 Course Objective
This course introduces fundamental principles of semiconductor devices, beginning with PN junctions and BJT operation, then focusing extensively on MOSFET device operation, CMOS digital design, and CMOS fabrication processes.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Understand the working of PN junction diode and MOS transistor operation.
- **CLO2**: Analyze static and dynamic characteristics of Resistive load Inverter and CMOS inverter.
- **CLO3**: Implement various CMOS logic circuits and transmission gates.
- **CLO4**: Illustrate the fabrication process and design rules for NMOS and CMOS circuits.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Introduction To Semiconductor: Energy bands, intrinsic/extrinsic semiconductors, p-n junction diode, Zener diode. BJT operation, biasing configurations, BJT switch, FET.
- NMOS & CMOS Process Technology: Masking sequence of NMOS and CMOS structures, latch-up in CMOS, electrical design rules, stick diagram and layout.
- MOS Transistor Theory: I-V characteristics, threshold voltage equation, body bias effect, short-channel effects, FinFET, GAA FETs, Nanosheet Transistors.
- Circuit Characterization: Resistive load inverter and CMOS inverter: static and dynamic characteristics, noise margin, power dissipation.
- Combinational Circuits: CMOS logic styles, realization of simple gates, complex logic circuits, transmission gates.
- Standards Familiarization: IEEE 1181-1991, IEEE 1801-2024.
