# UNC802: Prompt Engineering
**Semester**: Semester VIII | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: PEC | **L-T-P**: 2-0-2 | **Credits**: 3.0 | **Contact Hours**: 4

## 🎯 Course Objective
To provide students with a structured and practical understanding of Prompt Engineering, systematic input design for Large Language Models (LLMs), prompt patterns, optimization strategies, and responsible use of Generative AI.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Understand basic concepts of prompt engineering and its role in Generative AI systems.
- **CLO2**: Write clear and effective prompts using common prompting techniques.
- **CLO3**: Apply prompt design methods for structured and multi-step engineering tasks.
- **CLO4**: Evaluate AI-generated responses and demonstrate responsible and ethical use of Generative AI tools.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Introduction: Generative AI tools, prompt anatomy, role-based prompting, temperature and sampling parameters.
- Basic & Advanced Prompting: Zero-shot, Few-shot, Chain-of-Thought (CoT), Tree-of-Thought, Directional stimulus prompting, ReAct frameworks.
- Prompting for Complex Workflows: Decomposition, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) prompt patterns, structured outputs (JSON/YAML), constraints.
- Evaluation & Safety: Output validation, hallucination mitigation, jailbreak defense, ethical AI, privacy considerations.
- Standards Familiarization: IEEE 7000-2021, IEEE 7001-2021, IEEE 7002-2022, IEEE 1012-2016.
