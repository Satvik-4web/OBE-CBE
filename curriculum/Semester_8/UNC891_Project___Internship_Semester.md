# UNC891: Project / Internship Semester
**Semester**: Semester VIII | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: PRJ | **L-T-P**: 0-0-15 | **Credits**: 9.0 | **Contact Hours**: 15

## 🎯 Course Objective
To provide students with intensive full-semester industrial or capstone research experience, applying electronics and computer engineering skills to solve enterprise-level challenges.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Formulate and execute industry-relevant technical solutions applying electronics and computer engineering principles.
- **CLO2**: Implement, test, and document complex engineering artifacts adhering to professional engineering standards.
- **CLO3**: Demonstrate effective project management, oral defense, and professional ethics in workplace settings.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Full-time industry internship / sponsored research project / startup incubation. Engineering specification, design, execution, testing, mentor evaluation, technical report, and final presentation.

## 📖 Prescribed Text Books & References
1. Industry Internship and Project Guidelines, TIET (2025).
