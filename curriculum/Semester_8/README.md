## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) Quick Reference
### UNC801: Information Retrieval
- CLO1: Comprehend fundamental concepts and terminology associated with Information retrieval.
- CLO2: Understand and evaluate various types of indices and efficient storage of indices.
- CLO3: Evaluate scoring strategies and ranking models.
- CLO4: Analyze and implement advanced indexing, link analysis, and duplicate detection techniques for scalable web search.
- CLO5: Utilize machine learning methods for intelligent information retrieval.

### UNC802: Prompt Engineering
- CLO1: Understand basic concepts of prompt engineering and its role in Generative AI systems.
- CLO2: Write clear and effective prompts using common prompting techniques.
- CLO3: Apply prompt design methods for structured and multi-step engineering tasks.
- CLO4: Evaluate AI-generated responses and demonstrate responsible and ethical use of Generative AI tools.

### UNC891: Project / Internship Semester
- CLO1: Formulate and execute industry-relevant technical solutions applying electronics and computer engineering principles.
- CLO2: Implement, test, and document complex engineering artifacts adhering to professional engineering standards.
- CLO3: Demonstrate effective project management, oral defense, and professional ethics in workplace settings.
