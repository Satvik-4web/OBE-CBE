# UES104: Boot Camp
**Semester**: Semester I | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: BSC | **L-T-P**: 1-0-0 | **Credits**: 1.0 | **Contact Hours**: 1

## 🎯 Course Objective
To introduce first-year engineering students to collaborative engineering practices, hands-on maker skills, orientation with electronic and computing labs, and professional mindset.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Acquire orientation on engineering fundamentals and interdisciplinary tools.
- **CLO2**: Understand institutional ethics, collaborative workflows, and campus academic resources.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Hands-on workshop on engineering tools, prototyping foundations, version control basics, introduction to departmental laboratories and safety protocols.
- Design thinking sprints, problem formulation, teamwork dynamics, and exposure to emerging electronics and computing technologies.

1. Institution Induction and Maker Space Handbook, TIET (2025).
