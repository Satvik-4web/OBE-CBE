## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) Quick Reference
### UPH013: Physics
- CLO1: Understand damped and simple harmonic motion, the role of reverberation in designing a hall and generation and detection of ultrasonic waves.
- CLO2: Use Maxwell's equations to describe propagation of EM waves in a medium.
- CLO3: Demonstrate interference, diffraction and polarization of light.
- CLO4: Explain the working principle of Lasers.
- CLO5: Use the concept of wave function to find probability of a particle confined in a box.
- CLO6: Perform an experiment, collect data, tabulate and report them and interpret the results with error analysis.

### UES101: Engineering Drawing
- CLO1: Creatively comprehend the geometrical details of common engineering objects.
- CLO2: Draw dimensioned orthographic and isometric projections of simple engineering objects.
- CLO3: Interpret the meaning and intent of limits, fits and tolerances in the drawing.
- CLO4: Create/edit the engineering drawings for simple engineering objects using 2D drafting software.
- CLO5: Create/edit 3D models of engineering components using 3D modelling software.

### UHU003: Professional Communication
- CLO1: Apply communication concepts for effective interpersonal communication.
- CLO2: Speak assertively and effectively.
- CLO3: Interpret non-verbal cues in professional communication.
- CLO4: Write objectively, purposefully and effectively.
- CLO5: Design effective resumes and reports.

### UES102: Manufacturing Processes
- CLO1: Identify & analyse various machining processes/operations for manufacturing of industrial components.
- CLO2: Apply the basic principle of bulk and sheet metal forming operations.
- CLO3: Apply the knowledge of metal casting for different requirements.
- CLO4: Identify and analyse the requirements for achieving a sound welded joint and apply the concept of smart manufacturing.

### UMA022: Calculus for Engineers
- CLO1: Examine functions of several variables, define and compute partial derivatives, directional derivatives, and their use in finding maxima and minima in engineering problems.
- CLO2: Evaluate multiple integrals in Cartesian and Polar coordinates, and their applications to find areas and volumes.
- CLO3: Determine the convergence/divergence of infinite series, approximation of functions using power and series expansion and error estimation in engineering problems.
- CLO4: Represent complex numbers in Cartesian and Polar forms and test the analyticity of complex functions by using Cauchy Riemann equations.

### UES104: Boot Camp
- CLO1: Acquire orientation on engineering fundamentals and interdisciplinary tools.
- CLO2: Understand institutional ethics, collaborative workflows, and campus academic resources.
