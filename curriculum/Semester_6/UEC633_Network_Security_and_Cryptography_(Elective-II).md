# UEC633: Network Security and Cryptography (Elective-II)
**Semester**: Semester VI | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: PEC | **L-T-P**: 2-0-2 | **Credits**: 3.0 | **Contact Hours**: 4

## 🎯 Course Objective
To study cryptographic algorithms, public-key infrastructure, digital signatures, authentication protocols, and modern network intrusion defense mechanisms.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Understand cryptographic principles and classical ciphers.
- **CLO2**: Apply symmetric and asymmetric encryption algorithms to secure communications.
- **CLO3**: Implement hashing, digital signatures, and authentication protocols.
- **CLO4**: Analyze network vulnerabilities and design defense-in-depth security strategies.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Symmetric ciphers (DES, AES), Public-key cryptography (RSA, ECC), Hash functions (SHA-2/3), Digital signatures, Key exchange (Diffie-Hellman), IPsec, TLS, firewalls, zero-trust architecture.

## 📖 Prescribed Text Books & References
1. Stallings, William, Cryptography and Network Security: Principles and Practice, Pearson (2020).
