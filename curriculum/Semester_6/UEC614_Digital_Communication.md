# UEC614: Digital Communication
**Semester**: Semester VI | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: PCC | **L-T-P**: 3-1-2 | **Credits**: 4.5 | **Contact Hours**: 6

## 🎯 Course Objective
To build foundation for communication system design focusing on challenges of transmission/reception over physical channels in the presence of noise and interference.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Explain concepts of AM, FM, and digital pulse modulation.
- **CLO2**: Analyze pulse modulation techniques and baseband waveform shaping.
- **CLO3**: Perform statistical analysis of modulated waveforms from estimation and detection perspective.
- **CLO4**: Evaluate performance of digital modulation techniques operating over AWGN channels.
- **CLO5**: Analyze and apply error control coding in digital communication systems.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Introduction to Modulation: Sampling process, PAM, PCM, DPCM, Delta Modulation, Adaptive DM, TDM.
- Baseband Transmission: Line coding formats (NRZ, RZ, Manchester), ISI, Nyquist criterion, Correlative coding.
- Detection & Estimation: Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization, MAP criterion, maximum likelihood decision, matched filter receivers.
- Digital Modulation Techniques: ASK, FSK, BPSK, QPSK, M-ary QAM, MSK, bit error rate calculations over AWGN channel.
- Error Control Coding: Linear block codes, syndrome decoding, cyclic codes, convolutional codes, Viterbi decoding.
- Standards Familiarization: IEEE 1241-2023, IEEE 1658-2011, IEEE 802 Family.
