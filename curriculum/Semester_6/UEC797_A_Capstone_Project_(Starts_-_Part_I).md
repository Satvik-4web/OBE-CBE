# UEC797_A: Capstone Project (Starts - Part I)
**Semester**: Semester VI | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: PRJ | **L-T-P**: 1-0-2 | **Credits**: 1.0 | **Contact Hours**: 2

## 🎯 Course Objective
Culmination of undergraduate curriculum demonstrating students' attainment of program outcomes through solving real-world interdisciplinary problems.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Identify a technological need in society or industry and formulate it into a specific engineering problem.
- **CLO2**: Design and develop practical hardware, software, or integrated solutions using interdisciplinary engineering knowledge.
- **CLO3**: Recognize the importance of engineering solutions that consider safety, economic, environmental, and societal impacts.
- **CLO4**: Demonstrate effective teamwork by contributing meaningfully to group-based project tasks in a multidisciplinary environment.
- **CLO5**: Communicate technical ideas and project outcomes clearly and professionally with diverse audiences.
- **CLO6**: Assess engineering solutions with respect to ethical responsibilities adhering to professional canons.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Problem definition, specification development, literature survey, concept generation, concept evaluation, preliminary design, implementation strategy, and project planning.
