# UNC602: Theory of Computation
**Semester**: Semester VI | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: PCC | **L-T-P**: 3-1-0 | **Credits**: 3.5 | **Contact Hours**: 4

## 🎯 Course Objective
To introduce formal methods of computation, automata theory, formal languages, grammars, computability, and complexity classes.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Comprehend regular languages and finite automata and establish equivalence between regular expressions, NFAs, and DFAs.
- **CLO2**: Disambiguate context-free grammars and understand concepts of context-free languages and pushdown automata.
- **CLO3**: Analyse and design efficient Turing Machines.
- **CLO4**: Distinguish different computing languages and classify their respective computational complexity types.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Automata & Regular Expressions: DFA, NFA, epsilon transitions, regular expressions, pumping lemma, DFA minimization.
- Grammars & Pushdown Automata: Chomsky hierarchy, Context-free grammars, CNF, GNF, Pushdown Automata, ambiguity.
- Turing Machines: Turing machine models, Church-Turing thesis, Universal Turing Machines, Halting problem.
- Decidability & Complexity: Post Correspondence Problem, Rice's theorem, P, NP, and NP-complete complexity classes.
- Standards Familiarization: IEEE 42010-2011, IEEE 1012-2016, IEEE 1061-1998, IEEE 730-2014.
