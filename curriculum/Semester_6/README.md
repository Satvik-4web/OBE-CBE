## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) Quick Reference
### UEC614: Digital Communication
- CLO1: Explain concepts of AM, FM, and digital pulse modulation.
- CLO2: Analyze pulse modulation techniques and baseband waveform shaping.
- CLO3: Perform statistical analysis of modulated waveforms from estimation and detection perspective.
- CLO4: Evaluate performance of digital modulation techniques operating over AWGN channels.
- CLO5: Analyze and apply error control coding in digital communication systems.

### UEC615: Embedded Systems and IoT
- CLO1: Understand embedded system models using C++ language.
- CLO2: Understand internal architecture of ARM processor, register structure, and addressing modes.
- CLO3: Design ARM interfacing with memory, I/O devices, and peripherals using C++.
- CLO4: Analyze design aspects of hardware and software components of IoT including protocols.
- CLO5: Design interfaces for I/O devices, sensors, and communication modules.

### UNC602: Theory of Computation
- CLO1: Comprehend regular languages and finite automata and establish equivalence between regular expressions, NFAs, and DFAs.
- CLO2: Disambiguate context-free grammars and understand concepts of context-free languages and pushdown automata.
- CLO3: Analyse and design efficient Turing Machines.
- CLO4: Distinguish different computing languages and classify their respective computational complexity types.

### UEC633: Network Security and Cryptography (Elective-II)
- CLO1: Understand cryptographic principles and classical ciphers.
- CLO2: Apply symmetric and asymmetric encryption algorithms to secure communications.
- CLO3: Implement hashing, digital signatures, and authentication protocols.
- CLO4: Analyze network vulnerabilities and design defense-in-depth security strategies.

### UEC635: Blockchain Technology (Elective-III)
- CLO1: Explain concepts of distributed ledgers and blockchain architectures.
- CLO2: Analyze consensus algorithms including Proof of Work and Proof of Stake.
- CLO3: Develop and deploy smart contracts on modern blockchain platforms.
- CLO4: Evaluate enterprise blockchain use-cases in supply chain, identity, and finance.

### UNC603: Programming Practices Lab - I
- CLO1: Apply programming skills in Java/Python to solve computational problems.
- CLO2: Implement solutions using appropriate data structures and algorithmic techniques.
- CLO3: Develop logical reasoning and structured problem-solving ability through coding practice.
- CLO4: Perform timed programming tasks with attention to correctness and efficiency.

### UEC797_A: Capstone Project (Starts - Part I)
- CLO1: Identify a technological need in society or industry and formulate it into a specific engineering problem.
- CLO2: Design and develop practical hardware, software, or integrated solutions using interdisciplinary engineering knowledge.
- CLO3: Recognize the importance of engineering solutions that consider safety, economic, environmental, and societal impacts.
- CLO4: Demonstrate effective teamwork by contributing meaningfully to group-based project tasks in a multidisciplinary environment.
- CLO5: Communicate technical ideas and project outcomes clearly and professionally with diverse audiences.
- CLO6: Assess engineering solutions with respect to ethical responsibilities adhering to professional canons.
