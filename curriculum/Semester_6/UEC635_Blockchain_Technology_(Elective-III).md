# UEC635: Blockchain Technology (Elective-III)
**Semester**: Semester VI | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: PEC | **L-T-P**: 2-0-2 | **Credits**: 3.0 | **Contact Hours**: 4

## 🎯 Course Objective
To introduce distributed ledger technology, consensus mechanisms, smart contract development, and decentralized applications (DApps).

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Explain concepts of distributed ledgers and blockchain architectures.
- **CLO2**: Analyze consensus algorithms including Proof of Work and Proof of Stake.
- **CLO3**: Develop and deploy smart contracts on modern blockchain platforms.
- **CLO4**: Evaluate enterprise blockchain use-cases in supply chain, identity, and finance.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Distributed systems, cryptographic hash pointers, Merkle trees, Bitcoin protocol, Proof of Work/Stake, Ethereum, smart contracts with Solidity, decentralized finance, enterprise blockchains.

## 📖 Prescribed Text Books & References
1. Narayanan, A. et al., Bitcoin and Cryptocurrency Technologies, Princeton University Press (2016).
