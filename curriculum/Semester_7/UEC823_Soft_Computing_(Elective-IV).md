# UEC823: Soft Computing (Elective-IV)
**Semester**: Semester VII | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: PEC | **L-T-P**: 2-0-2 | **Credits**: 3.0 | **Contact Hours**: 4

## 🎯 Course Objective
To introduce fuzzy logic, neural networks, genetic algorithms, and hybrid soft computing systems capable of handling imprecision and uncertainty in engineering.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Understand theoretical foundations of fuzzy logic, neural networks, and evolutionary algorithms.
- **CLO2**: Design and implement fuzzy inference systems for engineering control problems.
- **CLO3**: Apply genetic algorithms to solve complex non-linear optimization tasks.
- **CLO4**: Develop hybrid intelligent systems combining neural and fuzzy approaches.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Fuzzy Sets & Logic: Membership functions, fuzzy relations, defuzzification, fuzzy rule-based inference.
- Neural Networks: Feedforward networks, backpropagation, radial basis functions, self-organizing maps.
- Evolutionary Computing: Genetic algorithms, chromosome encoding, crossover, mutation, fitness evaluation, multi-objective optimization.
- Hybrid Systems: Neuro-fuzzy systems (ANFIS), evolutionary neural networks, real-world control applications.
