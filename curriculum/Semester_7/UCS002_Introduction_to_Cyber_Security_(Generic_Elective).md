# UCS002: Introduction to Cyber Security (Generic Elective)
**Semester**: Semester VII | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: OEC | **L-T-P**: 2-0-0 | **Credits**: 2.0 | **Contact Hours**: 2

## 🎯 Course Objective
To introduce basic principles of cyber security, threats, malware, identity theft, web application security, and cyber laws.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Understand fundamental concepts of cyberspace, security threats, and vulnerabilities.
- **CLO2**: Apply security practices to safeguard personal and organizational digital assets.
- **CLO3**: Recognize legal and ethical implications under national and international cyber laws.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Cyber security landscape, attack vectors, social engineering, malware types, browser security, authentication best practices, IT Act 2000, digital privacy.

## 📖 Prescribed Text Books & References
1. Singer, P. W. and Friedman, Allan, Cybersecurity and Cyberwar: What Everyone Needs to Know, Oxford University Press.
