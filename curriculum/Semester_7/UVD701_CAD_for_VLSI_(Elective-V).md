# UVD701: CAD for VLSI (Elective-V)
**Semester**: Semester VII | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: PEC | **L-T-P**: 2-0-2 | **Credits**: 3.0 | **Contact Hours**: 4

## 🎯 Course Objective
To teach algorithmic methods for automated VLSI physical design, layout synthesis, placement, routing, and timing closure.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Explain electronic design automation (EDA) flows for VLSI circuits.
- **CLO2**: Implement partitioning, floorplanning, and placement algorithms.
- **CLO3**: Analyze routing algorithms and timing optimization techniques.
- **CLO4**: Use industry CAD tools to synthesize, simulate, and verify integrated circuits.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- VLSI design cycle, graph theory models for layout, partitioning (KL, FM algorithms), floorplanning, placement algorithms, global and detailed routing, clock tree synthesis.

## 📖 Prescribed Text Books & References
1. Sait, S. M. and Youssef, H., VLSI Physical Design Automation: Theory and Practice, World Scientific.
1. Sherwani, N. A., Algorithms for VLSI Physical Design Automation, Springer.
