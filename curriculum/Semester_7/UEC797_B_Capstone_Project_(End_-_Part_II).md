# UEC797_B: Capstone Project (End - Part II)
**Semester**: Semester VII | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: PRJ | **L-T-P**: 0-0-2 | **Credits**: 8.0 | **Contact Hours**: 2

## 🎯 Course Objective
Completion, prototyping, hardware/software validation, and public defense of the Capstone Project.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Formulate and implement end-to-end engineering solutions addressing real-world problems.
- **CLO2**: Validate engineered prototypes through rigorous experimental and simulation benchmarks.
- **CLO3**: Evaluate societal, safety, and environmental impacts of the engineered solution.
- **CLO4**: Demonstrate effective team leadership and collaborative workflow management.
- **CLO5**: Defend project deliverables through professional oral presentations and technical reports.
- **CLO6**: Uphold professional ethics and intellectual property standards in engineering practice.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Detailed design, simulation, prototyping, testing and validation, impact analysis, patent filing / paper publication, final report, oral examination.
