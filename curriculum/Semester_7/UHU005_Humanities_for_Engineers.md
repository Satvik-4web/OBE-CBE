# UHU005: Humanities for Engineers
**Semester**: Semester VII | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: HSS | **L-T-P**: 2-0-2 | **Credits**: 3.0 | **Contact Hours**: 4

## 🎯 Course Objective
To understand psychological, ethical, and economic foundations influencing individual and organizational behavior, ethical decision-making, and macroeconomic systems.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Improve understanding of human behavior with the help of interplay of professional, psychological and economic activities.
- **CLO2**: Apply knowledge of basic principles of psychology, economics and ethics for the solution of engineering problems.
- **CLO3**: Explain the impact of contemporary issues in psychology, economics and ethical principles on engineering.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Behavioral Foundations: Motives, emotions, cognition, emotional intelligence, group dynamics, Transactional Analysis.
- Human Values & Ethics: Values spectrum, moral development (Kohlberg), ethical frameworks (Kant), whistleblowing, CSR.
- Economic Perspective: Demand and supply, market structures, project appraisal, inflation, GDP, Globalization, WTO, GATT.
- Laboratory Work: Behavioral modification simulations, personality assessment, capital budgeting exercises, macroeconomic case studies.
