## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) Quick Reference
### UHU005: Humanities for Engineers
- CLO1: Improve understanding of human behavior with the help of interplay of professional, psychological and economic activities.
- CLO2: Apply knowledge of basic principles of psychology, economics and ethics for the solution of engineering problems.
- CLO3: Explain the impact of contemporary issues in psychology, economics and ethical principles on engineering.

### UEC823: Soft Computing (Elective-IV)
- CLO1: Understand theoretical foundations of fuzzy logic, neural networks, and evolutionary algorithms.
- CLO2: Design and implement fuzzy inference systems for engineering control problems.
- CLO3: Apply genetic algorithms to solve complex non-linear optimization tasks.
- CLO4: Develop hybrid intelligent systems combining neural and fuzzy approaches.

### UCS002: Introduction to Cyber Security (Generic Elective)
- CLO1: Understand fundamental concepts of cyberspace, security threats, and vulnerabilities.
- CLO2: Apply security practices to safeguard personal and organizational digital assets.
- CLO3: Recognize legal and ethical implications under national and international cyber laws.

### UVD701: CAD for VLSI (Elective-V)
- CLO1: Explain electronic design automation (EDA) flows for VLSI circuits.
- CLO2: Implement partitioning, floorplanning, and placement algorithms.
- CLO3: Analyze routing algorithms and timing optimization techniques.
- CLO4: Use industry CAD tools to synthesize, simulate, and verify integrated circuits.

### UNC703: Programming Practices Lab - II
- CLO1: Apply advanced programming techniques in Java/Python to complex algorithmic problems.
- CLO2: Implement tree and graph algorithms with optimal time and space complexity.
- CLO3: Formulate strategies for complex competitive programming and technical interviews.
- CLO4: Perform timed problem solving demonstrating code robustness and edge-case handling.

### UEC797_B: Capstone Project (End - Part II)
- CLO1: Formulate and implement end-to-end engineering solutions addressing real-world problems.
- CLO2: Validate engineered prototypes through rigorous experimental and simulation benchmarks.
- CLO3: Evaluate societal, safety, and environmental impacts of the engineered solution.
- CLO4: Demonstrate effective team leadership and collaborative workflow management.
- CLO5: Defend project deliverables through professional oral presentations and technical reports.
- CLO6: Uphold professional ethics and intellectual property standards in engineering practice.
