# UMA023: Differential Equations and Linear Algebra
**Semester**: Semester II | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: BSC | **L-T-P**: 3-1-0 | **Credits**: 3.5 | **Contact Hours**: 4

## 🎯 Course Objective
To introduce students the theory and concepts of differential equations, linear algebra, Laplace transformations and Fourier series which will equip them with adequate knowledge of mathematics to formulate and solve problems analytically.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Solve differential equations of first and 2nd order and basic application problems described by these equations.
- **CLO2**: Determine Laplace transforms and inverse Laplace transforms for various functions and solve initial and boundary value problems.
- **CLO3**: Determine Fourier series expansions of periodic functions and solve heat and wave equations.
- **CLO4**: Solve systems of linear equations using elementary row operations, compute bases/orthonormal bases, express linear transformation in matrix terms and find eigenvalues and eigenvectors.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Unit I: Review of first order differential equations, Second and higher order differential equations, Cauchy - Euler equation, Method of undetermined coefficients, variation of parameters, operator method, Applications.
- Unit II: Laplace transforms and its inverse, Properties of Laplace transforms, Unit step function, Impulse function, Initial and boundary value problems.
- Unit III: Fourier series on arbitrary intervals, Even and odd functions, Half range expansions, Partial differential equations, Separation of variables, Applications to heat/wave equations.
- Unit IV: Row reduced echelon form, Solution of system of linear equations, Matrix inversion, Linear spaces, Subspaces, Basis and dimension, Linear transformation and matrix representation, Eigen-values, Eigen-vectors and Diagonalisation.
