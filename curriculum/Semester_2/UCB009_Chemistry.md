# UCB009: Chemistry
**Semester**: Semester II | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: BSC | **L-T-P**: 3-0-2 | **Credits**: 4.0 | **Contact Hours**: 5

## 🎯 Course Objective
The course aims at elucidating principles of applied chemistry in industrial systems, water treatment, engineering materials, computational and analytical techniques.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Recognize principles and applications of atomic and molecular spectroscopy.
- **CLO2**: Explain the concepts of conductometric titrations, modern batteries and corrosion.
- **CLO3**: Apply and execute water quality parameter and treatment methods.
- **CLO4**: Discuss the concept of alternative fuels, application of polymers and SMILES.
- **CLO5**: Execute laboratory techniques like pH metry, potentiometry, spectrophotometry, conductometry and volumetry.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Atomic and Molecular spectroscopy: Introduction to spectroscopy, principles of atomic absorption, flame emission spectrophotometry and ICP-AES, Quantification by calibration method, Jablonski diagram, fluorescence and phosphorescence, Beer-Lambert's Law, UV-Vis and IR spectroscopy.
- Electrochemistry: Background of electrochemistry, Ionic mobility, Conductometric titrations, Modern Batteries: Pb-acid and Li ion battery, Corrosion and its protection.
- Water Treatment and Analysis: Physiochemical parameters of water quality, Softening of water: carbonate, phosphate, calgon and colloidal conditioning, Zeolite process, Ion exchange process, Desalination: Reverse osmosis & Electrodialysis.
- Fuels & Polymers: Classification of fuels, Calorific value, Cetane and Octane number, alternative fuels: biodiesel, Fuel cells: H2 production and storage. Classification of polymers, molecular weight calculations, conducting and biodegradable polymers.
- Computers in Chemistry: Introduction to SMILES (Simplified Molecular Input Line-Entry System): Methodology, encoding rules, notation-chemical structure interconversions.
