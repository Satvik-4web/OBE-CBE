## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) Quick Reference
### UCB009: Chemistry
- CLO1: Recognize principles and applications of atomic and molecular spectroscopy.
- CLO2: Explain the concepts of conductometric titrations, modern batteries and corrosion.
- CLO3: Apply and execute water quality parameter and treatment methods.
- CLO4: Discuss the concept of alternative fuels, application of polymers and SMILES.
- CLO5: Execute laboratory techniques like pH metry, potentiometry, spectrophotometry, conductometry and volumetry.

### UES103: Programming for Problem Solving
- CLO1: Comprehend and analyze the concepts of number system, memory, compilation and debugging of the programs in C language.
- CLO2: Analyze the control & iterative statements to solve problems with C language source codes.
- CLO3: Design and create programs for problem solving involving arrays, strings and pointers.
- CLO4: Evaluate and analyze programming concepts based on user-defined data types and file handling using C language.

### UES013: Electrical & Electronics Engineering
- CLO1: Apply various networks laws and theorems to solve dc circuits.
- CLO2: Compute different ac quantities with phasor representation.
- CLO3: Comprehend the operation in magnetic circuits, single phase transformer.
- CLO4: Recognize and apply number systems and simplification of Boolean expressions using Boolean algebra and K-Map.
- CLO5: Solve and implement logical functions using logic gates, decoders, and multiplexers.
- CLO6: Discuss and explain the working of Flip-Flops, diode, transistor and operational amplifier, their configurations and applications.

### UEN008: Energy and Environment
- CLO1: Comprehend the interdisciplinary context of environmental issues with reference to sustainability.
- CLO2: Assess the impact of anthropogenic activities on the various elements of environment and apply suitable techniques to mitigate their impact.
- CLO3: Demonstrate the application of technology in real time assessment and control of pollutants.
- CLO4: Correlate environmental concerns with conventional energy sources and assess the uses and limitations of non-conventional energy technologies.

### UMA023: Differential Equations and Linear Algebra
- CLO1: Solve differential equations of first and 2nd order and basic application problems described by these equations.
- CLO2: Determine Laplace transforms and inverse Laplace transforms for various functions and solve initial and boundary value problems.
- CLO3: Determine Fourier series expansions of periodic functions and solve heat and wave equations.
- CLO4: Solve systems of linear equations using elementary row operations, compute bases/orthonormal bases, express linear transformation in matrix terms and find eigenvalues and eigenvectors.
