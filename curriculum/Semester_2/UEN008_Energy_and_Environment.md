# UEN008: Energy and Environment
**Semester**: Semester II | **Program**: B.E. Electronics and Computer Engineering (ENC)
**Category**: BSC | **L-T-P**: 2-0-0 | **Credits**: 2.0 | **Contact Hours**: 2

## 🎯 Course Objective
The exposure to this course would facilitate the students in understanding the terms, definitions and scope of environmental and energy issues pertaining to current global scenario; understanding the need of sustainability in addressing the current environmental & energy challenges.

## 📋 Course Learning Objectives (CLO) / Course Outcomes (CO)
Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- **CLO1**: Comprehend the interdisciplinary context of environmental issues with reference to sustainability.
- **CLO2**: Assess the impact of anthropogenic activities on the various elements of environment and apply suitable techniques to mitigate their impact.
- **CLO3**: Demonstrate the application of technology in real time assessment and control of pollutants.
- **CLO4**: Correlate environmental concerns with conventional energy sources and assess the uses and limitations of non-conventional energy technologies.

## 📚 Syllabus & Topics
- Introduction: Concept of sustainability and sustainable use of natural resources, Climate Change & its related aspects.
- Air Pollution: Origin, Sources and effects of air pollution; meteorological parameters; wind roses; Atmospheric stability; Air Pollution Control Devices.
- Water Pollution: Sources of water pollution, water pollutants, Physicochemical characteristics, Components of wastewater treatment systems.
- Solid Waste Management: Sources, characteristics of municipal solid waste, Incineration, composting, landfilling.
- Energy Resources: Classification of Energy Resources; Non-conventional energy: Biomass energy, Solar energy-active and passive absorption systems; Collectors; Thermal and photo conversion.
